What we install and fix

One team for the roof and the plumbing, so your power and water systems work together.

Solar works

Rooftop solar installation, batteries and backup, sized to your daily use with proper mounting, cabling and inverters.

Solar water heating

Hot water for showers and kitchens without a heavy electricity bill.

Modern plumbing

Bathrooms, kitchens and full-house plumbing with wall-hung toilets, basins, baths and showers fitted neatly.

Elevated steel towers

Strong steel towers that lift your water tank for steady, gravity-fed pressure.

Water treatment

Filtration and treatment systems so the water at your tap is clean and safe.

Irrigation

Drip and sprinkler systems for farms and gardens, powered by solar where it makes sense.

Common questions

Do you visit before giving a quote?

For most jobs, yes. A short site visit lets us measure properly so the quote is accurate.

Can solar run my water pump?

Yes. We can size a solar system to power your pump so water keeps flowing without grid electricity.

Do you do small plumbing repairs?

Yes. We handle repairs and fittings as well as complete bathroom and kitchen installations.
